I am a theoretical physicist studying the non-equilibrium dynamics of interacting quantum many-body systems. I combine tensor-network methods with digital quantum simulation, running models of quantum walks and localization on IBM Quantum processors.

I am a Research Fellow at NTU Singapore, after postdoctoral positions at National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan, and TCG-CREST, Kolkata. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from IIT Guwahati in 2022.

I am interested in collaborations on quantum simulation, many-body physics, and quantum computing on NISQ platforms. Feel free to reach out for research discussions, invited talks, or academic opportunities.

Interests: digital quantum simulation · quantum walks & transport · flat-band and topological systems · localization · many-body dynamics · quantum machine learning
