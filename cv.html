Research Profile

Theoretical physicist working on non-equilibrium dynamics of interacting quantum many-body systems, combining tensor-network numerics (MPS, DMRG, TEBD, TDVP) with digital quantum simulation on superconducting NISQ hardware. My research line runs from interacting quantum walks, flux and localization in ladder geometries (Ph.D.) to executing these models on real quantum processors, with circuit compression and error mitigation (postdoc).
