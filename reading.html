Quantum Simulation & NISQ Computing

From Feynman's idea to today's hardware6 papers
  • Simulating physics with computers
    R. P. Feynman, Int. J. Theor. Phys. 21, 467 (1982)
    Where the idea of a quantum simulator began.
  • Universal quantum simulators
    S. Lloyd, Science 273, 1073 (1996)
    Shows that local Hamiltonians can be simulated efficiently with Trotterized circuits, the basis of digital quantum simulation.
  • Quantum simulation
    I. M. Georgescu, S. Ashhab, and F. Nori, Rev. Mod. Phys. 86, 153 (2014)
    A broad review of analog and digital quantum simulation.
  • Quantum computing in the NISQ era and beyond
    J. Preskill, Quantum 2, 79 (2018)
    The paper that named the NISQ era and set out what near-term devices can do.
  • Error mitigation for short-depth quantum circuits
    K. Temme, S. Bravyi, and J. M. Gambetta, Phys. Rev. Lett. 119, 180509 (2017)
    Introduces zero-noise extrapolation, now standard in hardware experiments.
  • Evidence for the utility of quantum computing before fault tolerance
    Y. Kim et al., Nature 618, 500 (2023)
    Large-scale many-body dynamics on an IBM processor with error mitigation.

Tensor-Network Methods

MPS, DMRG, TEBD and TDVP4 papers
  • A practical introduction to tensor networks: Matrix product states and projected entangled pair states
    R. Orús, Ann. Phys. 349, 117 (2014)
    The friendliest entry point to tensor networks.
  • The density-matrix renormalization group in the age of matrix product states
    U. Schollwöck, Ann. Phys. 326, 96 (2011)
    The standard reference for DMRG and MPS algorithms.
  • Efficient simulation of one-dimensional quantum many-body systems
    G. Vidal, Phys. Rev. Lett. 93, 040502 (2004)
    Introduces TEBD for real-time evolution.
  • Unifying time evolution and optimization with matrix product states
    J. Haegeman et al., Phys. Rev. B 94, 165116 (2016)
    The TDVP algorithm for MPS.

Quantum Walks

Theory and the first correlated-walk experiment2 papers
  • Universal computation by quantum walk
    A. M. Childs, Phys. Rev. Lett. 102, 180501 (2009)
    Why continuous-time quantum walks matter for computation.
  • Strongly correlated quantum walks in optical lattices
    P. M. Preiss et al., Science 347, 1229 (2015)
    Experiment showing interacting two-particle quantum walks and bound pairs.

Localization & Flat Bands

Anderson, many-body localization, compact states3 papers
  • Absence of diffusion in certain random lattices
    P. W. Anderson, Phys. Rev. 109, 1492 (1958)
    The original paper on Anderson localization.
  • Colloquium: Many-body localization, thermalization, and entanglement
    D. A. Abanin, E. Altman, I. Bloch, and M. Serbyn, Rev. Mod. Phys. 91, 021001 (2019)
    A readable overview of localization in interacting systems.
  • Artificial flat band systems: from lattice models to experiments
    D. Leykam, A. Andreanov, and S. Flach, Adv. Phys. X 3, 1473052 (2018)
    Compact localized states and flat-band lattices.

Quantum Hardware Milestones

Error correction and logical qubits3 papers
  • Quantum error correction below the surface code threshold
    Google Quantum AI and Collaborators, Nature 638, 920 (2025)
    Logical error rates that fall as the code grows: a key step towards fault tolerance.
  • Logical quantum processor based on reconfigurable atom arrays
    D. Bluvstein et al., Nature 626, 58 (2024)
    Algorithms run on dozens of logical qubits with neutral atoms.
  • Phase transitions in random circuit sampling
    A. Morvan et al., Nature 634, 328 (2024)
    Shows when noise wins and when a quantum processor stays beyond classical reach.

Many-Body Physics on Quantum Processors

New phases and bound states on real devices3 papers
  • Time-crystalline eigenstate order on a quantum processor
    X. Mi et al., Nature 601, 531 (2022)
    A discrete time crystal realized on a superconducting chip.
  • Formation of robust bound states of interacting microwave photons
    A. Morvan et al., Nature 612, 240 (2022)
    Interacting photons form bound states that survive integrability breaking, closely related to pairing in quantum walks.
  • Measurement-induced entanglement and teleportation on a noisy quantum processor
    Google Quantum AI and Collaborators, Nature 622, 481 (2023)
    Measurement-induced phases observed directly on hardware.

Quantum Walks on Superconducting Qubits

Correlated walks on programmable chips2 papers
  • Strongly correlated quantum walks with a 12-qubit superconducting processor
    Z. Yan et al., Science 364, 753 (2019)
    Interacting two-particle walks and fermionization on a qubit chain.
  • Quantum walks on a programmable two-dimensional 62-qubit superconducting processor
    M. Gong et al., Science 372, 948 (2021)
    Single- and two-particle walks in two dimensions with interference control.

Quantum vs. Classical Simulation

Where is the real quantum advantage?3 papers
  • Practical quantum advantage in quantum simulation
    A. J. Daley et al., Nature 607, 667 (2022)
    A clear perspective on where quantum simulators can beat classical methods.
  • Efficient tensor network simulation of IBM's Eagle kicked Ising experiment

  • Fast and converged classical simulations of evidence for the utility of quantum computing before fault tolerance

    Another classical answer to the same experiment, using Pauli-path methods.
