Quantum Playbook

Learn quantum computing by playing, with Qubi as your guide. Every chapter has interactive widgets running a real quantum simulation, the stories of the physicists behind the ideas, and a quiz that explains every mistake.

Made for undergraduates up to postdocs who have never worked on quantum. No quantum background needed; the maths is always one click away.
